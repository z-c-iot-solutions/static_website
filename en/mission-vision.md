    <h2>Mission & Vision</h2>
    <p class="lede">
      Two short statements — one about what we do today, one about the
      network we're trying to build.
    </p>

    <div class="grid-2" style="margin-top:36px;">
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Mission</h3>
        <p>
          Make long-range, low-power sensor networks accessible to teams
          that don't have an in-house radio engineer — through clear
          consultancy, hands-on training, and secure-by-default designs
          built on mioty and LoRaWAN.
        </p>
      </div>
      <div class="card">
        <h3>Vision</h3>
        <p>
          A future where every field, factory, and city block that needs to
          report a measurement can do so cheaply, securely, and for years on
          a single battery — and where the people building that future learn
          from each other in the open.
        </p>
      </div>
